# THISO Leasing Email Design System

Status: **IMPLEMENTATION COMPLETE under CR-119; release conditional on browser visual QA and EMAIL-ASSET-01**
Applies to: transactional and operational email generated by the Leasing & Mall Operations Platform.

## Purpose

Every email should answer, in this order:

1. What happened?
2. How urgent is it?
3. Which object is affected?
4. When is the relevant date or deadline?
5. What should the recipient do next?

The design system changes presentation and the safety of the rendering boundary. It does not define notification eligibility, schedules, recipients, approval rules, financial formulas, state transitions, or permissions.

## Brand usage

- Preferred static asset: `apps/frontend/public/logo.png`, the THISO symbol and wordmark already used by the application shell.
- White-card use only. The dark wordmark remains visible and the transparent background avoids a boxed logo treatment.
- Render at 112 × 41 CSS pixels with `alt="THISO"`, matching the asset ratio.
- Do not redraw the logo, convert it to a new mark, or use a generated approximation.
- `logo2.png` is a large near-white canvas and is unsuitable for a compact email header. `logo3.png` is a wordmark-only fallback.
- Per BC-025, transactional email always uses the repository `logo.png`; runtime `BrandingSettings.logoUrl` never overrides it.
- Use an absolute HTTPS URL in deployed environments. `FRONTEND_URL` must be production-correct and publicly reachable by mail clients.
- Checked UAT asset endpoint: `https://uatls.thisoretail.store/logo.png` returned the exact repository PNG publicly on 2026-09-08. EMAIL-ASSET-01 still requires confirmation that the deployed backend environment sets this frontend origin.

## Canvas

- Outer background: `#F5F6F8`.
- Content card: `#FFFFFF`, fluid width, `max-width: 640px`.
- Desktop outer padding: about 28px vertical and 12px horizontal.
- Card content padding: 32px desktop, 20px below 620px.
- Avoid a giant colored brand banner. Use a compact white header separated by a subtle border.
- Core layout uses presentation tables, explicit cell padding, and inline styles.

## Typography

Font stack: `Arial, Helvetica, "Segoe UI", "Helvetica Neue", sans-serif`.

| Role | Size | Weight | Line height |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Eyebrow/category | 11px | 700 | 16px |
| Main title | 23px | 700 | 31px |
| Body/description | 15px | 400 | 23px |
| Metadata label | 13px | 400 | 20px |
| Metadata value | 14px | 700 when important | 20px |
| Hero metric | 38px | 700 | 44px |
| CTA | 14px | 700 | 16px |
| Footer/fallback URL | 12px | 400 | 18–19px |

Do not load web fonts. Templates and tests must preserve Vietnamese UTF-8 text.

## Severity system

Severity is a visual mapping of existing business state, never a source of business rules.

| Severity | Accent | Tint | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `INFO` | `#1249BF` | `#EEF3FD` | Normal information or action without an established warning state |
| `SUCCESS` | `#04724D` | `#EBFBF3` | Approved, completed, paid, or verified |
| `WARNING` | `#9A5B06` | `#FEF6E7` | Approaching deadline or operational attention |
| `CRITICAL` | `#B3261E` | `#FDEDEC` | Overdue, failed, expired, or urgent escalation |

- Always pair color with a readable status word.
- Red is reserved for critical states, not general branding.
- Contract expiry preserves the existing renderer split: over 60 days INFO, 31–60 WARNING, 30 or fewer CRITICAL. The scheduler's actual send thresholds remain 180/90/60/30 days.
- Fitout SLA breach is WARNING; configured escalation is CRITICAL.
- Dunning remains governed by its policy/overdue state; template styling does not alter policy levels.

## Standard structure

1. Hidden preheader.
2. Three-pixel semantic severity rule.
3. Compact brand header: logo left, `LEASING PLATFORM` right.
4. Event header: category, title, worded badge, concise description.
5. Optional hero metric.
6. Optional object summary table.
7. Primary CTA.
8. Optional trusted guidance.
9. Standard footer.

Not every email needs a hero, summary, or CTA. Account and SMTP-test messages are valid simpler variants.

## Rendering components

- `EmailLayout` / `renderEmail`: document shell and fixed reading order.
- `EmailHeader`: approved logo and compact product label.
- `EmailEventHeader`: category, title, description, severity badge.
- `EmailSeverityBadge`: semantic word plus color.
- `EmailHeroMetric`: one event-defining number/amount and its unit.
- `EmailInfoTable`: label/value rows with missing optional rows removed.
- `EmailCTA`: table-cell button plus readable literal fallback URL.
- `EmailDivider`: one-pixel table divider.
- `EmailFooter`: automatic-email statement and optional environment label.

Names may follow the backend's function-based TypeScript architecture; React components are not appropriate for the current renderer.

## Object summary

- Use a real two-column presentation table.
- Labels are muted; values are strong and right-aligned on desktop.
- At narrow widths, label and value stack without horizontal scrolling.
- Omit null, undefined, and empty optional rows.
- Never print placeholders such as `undefined`, fake Mall/owner names, or invented contact details.
- Required-field absence should be asserted by tests and logged by the producer where operationally appropriate.

## Subject convention

- Production: `[THISO Leasing] {event-specific subject}`.
- Non-production: `[{APP_ENV}][THISO Leasing] {event-specific subject}`.
- `APP_ENV` absent, `production`, or `prod` produces no environment prefix.
- Do not hand-code `[TEST]`, `[UAT]`, or numbered test markers in individual templates.
- Keep internal technical terminology out of tenant-facing subjects.

## Preheader

- Adds object/date context not already conveyed by the subject.
- Example: `HD-2026-0088 · Công ty TNHH ABC · hết hạn 30/09/2026`.
- Hide visually with email-compatible inline styles and `mso-hide:all`.
- Escape all dynamic values.

## CTA and deep links

- CTA label describes the destination: `Xem hợp đồng`, `Xem đề xuất`, `Xem hóa đơn`, `Xem ticket`.
- Approximate touch height: 44px.
- Use an inline-styled table cell for the colored background; do not depend on an anchor background alone.
- Print the literal URL below the button as a fallback.
- Entity CTAs open authenticated detail pages. No approval, payment, rejection, deletion, or state-changing action is performed directly from an email.
- Verified parameter contracts are recorded in `docs/EMAIL_TEMPLATE_INVENTORY.md`.
- Fitout submittal and Tenant Portal inspection currently lack record-level route parameters; use their list/root destinations until a separately reviewed frontend route contract exists.

## Responsive and Outlook rules

- Use nested tables with `role="presentation"`, `cellpadding="0"`, `cellspacing="0"`, and `border="0"`.
- Put critical spacing, backgrounds, borders, font sizes, and colors inline.
- The layout must be readable without the media query; media rules are progressive enhancement only.
- Do not depend on flexbox, grid, absolute positioning, background images, external CSS, advanced selectors, or web fonts.
- At `max-width: 620px`, remove card side borders/radius, reduce padding to 20px, and stack information labels/values.
- Long tenant names and contract numbers must wrap inside cells without widening the card.

## Dark mode

- Declare a light color scheme and use explicit backgrounds and text colors.
- Do not introduce a separate dark-mode composition until the current renderer has reliable client support.
- Logo alt text and the worded severity badge preserve meaning when images or colors are altered by the client.

## Accessibility

- Maintain at least 4.5:1 contrast for normal text on its background.
- Do not convey severity by color alone.
- Use descriptive CTA labels and logo alt text.
- Keep logical DOM reading order identical to visual order.
- Avoid body text below 13px and primary content below 14px.
- Presentation tables must not be announced as data tables.

## HTML safety

- Escape `&`, `<`, `>`, `"`, and `'` for every business/user-supplied string.
- Escape attribute values, including CTA URLs.
- Trusted template copy may contain limited markup such as `<strong>` only when dynamic fragments inside it are escaped first.
- Do not allow arbitrary HTML from tenant names, ticket subjects, comments, unit codes, or uploaded content.
- Activation tokens stay in the existing HTTPS link; do not echo them elsewhere in the message.

## Currency

- Money is always amount plus explicit source currency.
- Templates must not call a formatter that defaults absent currency to VND.
- Supported authoritative codes are VND, USD, and MMK via the existing `CurrencyCode` model.
- If currency context is unexpectedly absent, render an explicit unknown-currency marker or block/log the render; never label it VND.
- Do not infer currency from locale or current Mall settings.
- Do not aggregate or convert values in an email template.

## Dates and time

- Vietnam-facing business dates: `DD/MM/YYYY`.
- Use `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh` only where a time is useful, such as the SMTP test.
- Do not expose raw ISO timestamps.
- Date formatting is presentation-only; it must not alter deadline calculations.

## Footer

Use:

> THISO Leasing Platform
> Email được gửi tự động từ hệ thống. Vui lòng không trả lời email này.

Show a configuration-derived environment marker in non-production. Do not invent addresses, legal entities, postal details, or monitored mailbox claims.

## Template families

- Deadline / expiry: contract expiry, Fitout SLA.
- Approval: proposal approval, Fitout submittal approval.
- Finance: invoice issued, invoice overdue.
- Operations: ticket SLA escalation, inspection notice.
- System/security: Tenant Portal activation/reset, SMTP configuration test.

No reachable production templates were found for payment received/failed, proposal approved/rejected email, work-order assignment email, service-contract email, or SAP failure email as of the inventory date.

## Preview and test requirements

Preview fixtures must be non-sensitive and include:

- Contract expiry.
- Approval required.
- Proposal approved (BLOCKED until a production event/template contract exists; may be a visual-only family fixture but must not be described as production reachable).
- Invoice issued.
- Invoice overdue.
- Payment received (same constraint as proposal approved).
- Ticket/work-order assigned (Ticket can represent the Operations family; Work Order is not production-email reachable).

Render desktop and mobile screenshots without sending SMTP email. Test Vietnamese accents; long names/numbers; absent optional fields; explicit VND, USD, and MMK; HTML injection attempts; subject environment prefixes; expiry edge cases; and plaintext. CR-119 adopted automatic plaintext generation for direct sends and backward-compatible optional plaintext persistence for queued sends.
