# Quick Guide — Tenant (Tenant Portal)

**Note on format:** text-only, no screenshots — see `01-sales-crm.md`'s note.

## What do I use this for?

Viewing your own contract, invoices, and (if applicable) fitout/
submittal status, and submitting requests (tickets) to the mall
management team.

## What can I see?

- Your own Contract(s) and their key dates/terms.
- Your own Invoices and payment history.
- Your own Fitout project's progress, if you're currently fitting out a
  unit.
- You will **never** see another tenant's contracts, invoices, or fitout
  data — this is enforced by the system, not just hidden in the menus.

## Critical workflow

```text
Log in with your tenant-portal account
→ View invoices, download documents, check fitout progress
→ Submit a Ticket for anything you need from mall management
   (maintenance, questions, requests)
```

## Common errors

- **"Bạn không có quyền truy cập" (you don't have access)** on a link —
  this usually means the link was for someone else's record; if you
  believe you're seeing this on your own data in error, contact support
  with the exact page/link and a Reference ID if shown.
- **Can't see a document** — some document types aren't yet available
  through the tenant portal even if they exist for staff; contact your
  mall management contact if you need something specific.

## Where to get support

Contact your mall management representative directly, or use the Ticket
feature in the portal — do not use the internal staff support channels
listed in `docs/golive/SUPPORT_MODEL.md`, which are for staff use.
